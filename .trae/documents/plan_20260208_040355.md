### 2. Synchronization Service (SyncService)

* **Two-Way Sync**:

* **Proof Upload**:

  * Integrated `ImagePicker` in `TransactionFormPage`.

  * Implemented `_uploadProof` in `SyncService` to upload images to Supabase Storage (`proofs` bucket) and link them to transactions.
